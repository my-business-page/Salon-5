THE LIVING ROOM EXPERIENCE

Less rush.
More attention.

Public profiles describe a cosy, private studio led by Mae, with an emphasis on personalised consultation and professional hairstyling.

01 · PERSONAL

Led by Mae

Public business information identifies Mae as the owner and describes more than a decade of hairstyling experience. The studio's proposition is personal attention rather than a crowded salon-floor feeling.

02 · TAILORED

Made to fit

Colour, cuts and texture work start with the person wearing the result. Face shape, natural texture, lifestyle and maintenance expectations all matter.

03 · CARE

Condition first

Previous bleach, colour and chemical work can change what is sensible. A consultation gives the studio a chance to assess the canvas before committing to a technique.

04 · ATMOSPHERE

A quieter room

The studio is publicly described as a cosy loft-style space around Beauty World, close to Beauty World MRT. It is designed to feel more private and considered.
